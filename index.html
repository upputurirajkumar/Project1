Strategic Context

Churn is not just a prediction problem. It's a decision problem.

Traditional churn projects stop at generating an accuracy score. Operational telecom environments require capacity-constrained prioritization that directly protects high-value recurring revenue.

The Operational Vulnerability

The Commercial Cascade

Why reactive customer loss degrades enterprise margin.

01

Customer Leaves Unannounced

Subscribers churn silently without filing complaints, switching directly to competing carriers.

↓
02

Compounding Revenue Impact

High-ARPU multi-line and fiber accounts trigger immediate contraction in Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).

↓
03

Compressed Retention Window

Intervention is only viable during pre-cancellation signals, before contract expiry or port-out completion.

↓
04

Need to Quantify Honest Risk

Raw classification labels (0 or 1) fail to convey true uncertainty or probabilistic urgency.

↓
05

Need to Prioritize Action Under Capacity

Call centers and retention budgets cannot contact 48,000 churners simultaneously; outreach must be ranked.

Conventional Approach

Pure Churn Classification

  • × Treats a $20/mo user the same as a $120/mo enterprise bundle user if both have 60% churn probability.
  • × Generates massive list of unranked flags that easily overwhelm retention teams.
  • × Lacks accounting for contract timing; flags customers who still have 22 months remaining on contract.
  • × Measures offline accuracy without commercial capacity constraints.
No-Churn Telecom V2

Retention Intelligence System

  • ✓ Synthesizes 3 Critical Factors: 50% calibrated churn risk, 30% monthly billing exposure, and 20% contract urgency.
  • ✓ Capacity-Aware Routing: Selects the optimal top N accounts that maximize protected recurring revenue under weekly operational budgets.
  • ✓ Actionable Archetype Assignment: Distinguishes high-value emergency retention from low-touch automated loyalty offers.
  • ✓ Transparent Governance: Fully auditable scoring rules that eliminate black-box decision confusion.
Prioritization Model

Retention Priority Formula

Every customer is assigned a unified Priority Index derived from three validated operational dimensions.

50%
Churn Risk
Calibrated machine learning churn probability output from the champion Random Forest model.
30%
Monthly Charges
Normalized monthly billing magnitude ($20–$120) prioritizing accounts whose attrition creates maximum MRR loss.
20%
Contract Urgency
Vulnerability index based on contract status (month-to-month contracts trigger highest flight risk).
Priority Index = (0.50 × ChurnRisk) + (0.30 × NormMonthlyCharges) + (0.20 × ContractUrgency)

Engineering Timeline

Project Execution Journey

From initial business framing to deployed retention decision intelligence.

PHASE 01 • PROBLEM FRAMING

Defining the Decision Bottleneck

Identified that naive binary classification fails to resolve finite retention call-center bandwidth. Structured retention prioritization as a joint risk-value optimization problem.

PHASE 02 • DATA INGESTION & HYGIENE

Audit of 243,553 Records

Validated 0 missing values, 0 duplicate records, and established an 80/20 stratified partition (seed 42). Excluded 213,442 pincodes to prevent high-cardinality memorization.

PHASE 03 • EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS

Class Imbalance & Tenurial Patterns

Quantified 20.0478% churn baseline across 48,827 churners. Isolated contract tenure and fiber internet connection as primary drivers of subscription vulnerability.

PHASE 04 • FEATURE ENGINEERING

Transforming 14 Raw Attributes to 82 Features

Engineered one-hot encodings for payment modes, scaled monthly billing figures, and generated multi-service bundling indicators.

PHASE 05 • MODEL BENCHMARKING

Evaluating Candidate Algorithms

Benchmarked linear classifiers, Extra Trees, and Hist Gradient Boosting against Random Forest. Selected tuned Random Forest (100 trees, depth 10) as champion.

PHASE 06 • PROBABILITY CALIBRATION

Platt Scaling on 24,356 Holdout Samples

Fitted Sigmoid Platt scaling to transform raw decision tree vote distributions into honest empirical probabilities, achieving a verified Brier Score of 0.1600.

PHASE 07 • RISK INTELLIGENCE

Formulating Decision Prioritization

Built the 50% Churn Risk + 30% Monthly Charges + 20% Contract Urgency weighting framework to rank customer outreach lists by financial risk impact.

PHASE 08 • RETENTION DECISION SYSTEM

Capacity Simulation & Portfolio Deployment

Integrated capacity-constrained campaign simulation and delivered the complete static portfolio showcase interface with zero external runtime dependencies.
